# /brag plan — Cortex

**What it is:** one native Mac app that replaces Launchpad and upgrades the Dock, the notch and how your music looks — plus a ⌘K bar.
**For:** Mac people who live in the Dock and the menu bar and want it all to feel like theirs.
**Sets it apart:** four things at once, in one free app, native and private — and it looks like 2026 (Liquid Glass, LaunchMe-grade widgets).
**Hook:** the four words fire on the beat, each one a pillar: Launchpad. Dock. Notch. Music. → "One app."
**Visual identity:** near-black #0b0b0f, white display type (Inter Display 800, tight tracking), the site's sunset/ocean bands, real screenshots.
**Tone:** default (punchy, clean). **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, ~21.3 s.
**Music:** "Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 10" — Sascha Ende (CC BY 4.0), ~110 BPM, beat ≈ 0.545 s; cuts on beats.
