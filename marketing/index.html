Questions

Good to know

Does anything leave my computer?

No. Files, OCR, ranking and the LLM all run locally. There's no account, no server, no telemetry.

Won't screen recording fill my disk?

No — Cortex runs OCR in memory and stores only the extracted text. A full day costs a few hundred KB, not gigabytes. Screenshots are never written.

What do I need for the AI features?

Ollama with a local model (e.g. llama3.1:8b). Cortex auto-picks the best one installed.

Is it really free?

Yes — free and open-source. Bring your own machine; everything runs on it.
