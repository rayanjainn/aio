Privacy

Local. Paused in
one keystroke.

Stays on your Mac

Indexes live in ~/.cortex. No account, no cloud sync. Weather only calls Open-Meteo for the city you choose.

Never sees secrets

Password fields, 1Password, Keychain, Signal and banking apps are never captured. Add your own blocklist.

⌘⇧P, anywhere

A global hotkey and menu-bar switch stop capture instantly — the REC dot tells you at a glance.
