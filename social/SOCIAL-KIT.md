## Read this first

1. **The film is a recreation, not screen-recorded footage.** It is built from the website's interactive demo. Say so
   ("interactive demo of the UI") and post real screen recordings of the actual app too (⌘⇧5 on your Mac). People who
   download the app and find it looks different will leave bad comments, and the platforms' viewers can tell.
2. **The first-launch macOS warning will cost you most of your installs.** AIO isn't notarized, so a viewer sees
   "Apple could not verify…". Put the two-step fix in every description and pinned comment (text below). Notarizing
   ($99/year Apple Developer account) is the fix that moves conversion the most.
3. **"AIO" is a crowded name.** Expect most obvious handles to be taken, and search results full of other products.
   Pick a handle that says what it is (below), and treat a trademark check as something to do before you spend money on it.
4. **One post won't do anything.** Plan on a post a day for two weeks, then three a week, and real-app recordings as
   the main content. Free Mac utilities grow slowly [Likely].
